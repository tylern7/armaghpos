Armagh’s consultants come from grocery, retail, and restaurant operations. They will spec the system — and tell you if a cheaper one is the wrong one.

On-demand video classes, hands-on orientation, and Q&A so staff learn the system without shutting the store. On-site and in-house training are available when a classroom video is not enough.

Preventative maintenance

Downtime costs more than a maintenance visit. Scheduled service covers virus and spyware scans, log checks, Windows updates, drive care, and confirmation that backups are actually finishing.

ArmGuard cloud backup

If you cannot say how the POS is backed up, assume it is not. ArmGuard installs on the hardware, runs after close, and sends critical POS data offsite — so a fire, flood, or dead drive is not the end of your item file.

Credit & debit processing

Integrated processing, not a separate terminal your staff re-key into. Visa, Mastercard, American Express, debit, and contactless wallets including Apple Pay and Google Pay. Armagh technicians are QIR-certified with the PCI Security Standards Council. Chase Paymentech is one of the integrated options.

Leasing, rental & finance

Pay over time so the system can start paying for itself before the capital is gone. Armagh helps place traditional equipment leasing or financing without burning the operating line of credit you need for inventory.

Managed network security

A firewall appliance in front of the POS network, rather than hoping each terminal protects itself. Aimed at viruses, intrusion, malware, ransomware, and the rest of what shows up when card data is on the line.

Questions

Before you fill in the form.

Which payment methods can the systems accept?

Major cards including Visa, Mastercard, and American Express, plus debit. Contactless wallets such as Apple Pay and Google Pay are supported. The useful part is integration: the amount travels from the POS to the pinpad, so staff are not re-keying it.

Can we sell online as well as in the store?

Yes. eCommerce and online ordering can sit beside the in-store system so payments, inventory, and order fulfillment are not a second, disconnected shop. Restaurant online ordering, kiosk, and scan-to-order are part of the Genius lineup; Catapult has WebCart for retail.

Is this only for large chains?

No. Armagh still sells a straightforward cash register when that is the whole job, and scales the same software from a one-location shop or quick-service counter up to multi-lane grocery. The consultation is how you avoid buying the chain system for a single counter.

How is card data protected?

Integrated payments, encryption, and PCI DSS practices are the baseline. Technicians are QIR-certified with the PCI Security Standards Council. Managed network hardware can sit in front of the POS so each terminal is not its own firewall. No system removes the need for sensible passwords and staff process — Armagh will say that in the consult.

Do you handle gift cards and loyalty?

Yes. Physical and digital gift cards, balance checks at the till, and loyalty tools for repeat visits. Catapult can host the gift-card balances yourself, which avoids inactivity fees from a third-party card company. Card stock can be ordered through Armagh and printed to the store’s look.

Do you supply the hardware, or only software?

Both. Registers, touch terminals, printers, payment devices, mobile POS, self-checkout, scales, electronic shelf labels, and ATMs. Software without a serviceable lane is how stores end up with a pretty screen and a dead printer.

What does it do besides take the payment?

Inventory, employee controls, sales reporting, scale management, online orders, and connections to accounting or delivery platforms where the chosen POS supports them. The right list depends on the business — a bar and a supermarket should not get the same checklist.

Do you still sell ordinary cash registers?

Yes. The company opened as Armagh Cash Register. Box-style electronic registers, including the Sam4S SPS-340 and SPS-345, are still sold and serviced.
